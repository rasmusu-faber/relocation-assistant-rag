# Registering family members for health insurance

If you are insured (through work, self-employment, or voluntary insurance) you can
extend NFZ coverage to close **family members who have no insurance title of their
own** — at no extra contribution.

## Who counts as a family member

Typically a non-working spouse, and children (usually up to 18, or longer while
still in education). A dependent parent or grandparent in the same household can
also qualify in some cases.

## How to register them

You notify your insurer that you want to add a family member:

- **Employees:** ask your employer to submit the **ZUS ZCNA** form.
- **Self-employed or voluntarily insured:** you submit **ZUS ZCNA** yourself.

Registration is free — family members do not pay a separate contribution; they are
covered under yours.

## What they need

Each family member needs a PESEL number to be registered and to use NFZ care.

## Note

This adds dependents to *your* insurance. It does not apply to a spouse who has
their own title to insurance (e.g. their own job).
