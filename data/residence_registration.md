# Residence registration (zameldowanie) in Poland

Zameldowanie is the registration of your place of residence with the local
municipal office. It is separate from the EU residence registration of stay.

## Registering your address (zameldowanie)

- Done at the municipal office (Urząd Gminy / Urząd Miasta) for your address.
- You typically need a passport/ID and a document proving your right to the
  dwelling (e.g. a tenancy agreement or owner confirmation).
- Registration is free and can often be combined with a PESEL application.

## EU residence registration of stay

EU citizens staying in Poland longer than 3 months should register their stay at
the regional Voivodeship Office (Urząd Wojewódzki). For the Pomeranian region
(including Gdańsk) this is the Pomorski Urząd Wojewódzki.

Documents commonly required:

- Valid passport or national ID card.
- Proof of the purpose of stay (employment contract, enrolment, or proof of
  sufficient funds and health insurance).
- A completed registration-of-stay application form.

After registration you receive a registration certificate confirming your right
of residence as an EU citizen.
