# Health insurance through employment (umowa o pracę)

Public healthcare in Poland is run by the National Health Fund (NFZ), and access
is tied to paying a health contribution (składka zdrowotna). This page covers the
case where you are employed on an employment contract.

## Who registers you

When you start a job on an umowa o pracę, your **employer** registers you with
ZUS (Social Insurance Institution) using the ZUS ZUA form, normally within 7 days
of your start date. You do not file this yourself.

## What is paid

The employer calculates and transfers your monthly health contribution to ZUS,
which forwards it to the NFZ. From the moment you are registered you are entitled
to NFZ care.

## What you need

A PESEL number is required for the registration — bring it and your ID to your
employer's HR/payroll when you start.

## Note

This covers you as the insured person. A non-working spouse or your children are
not covered automatically; they must be registered separately as family members.
