# Address registration (zameldowanie)

Zameldowanie is registering the **address where you live** with the local
municipal office. It records where you reside — it is separate from the EU
registration of stay and from getting a PESEL.

## Where and how

- Done at the **municipal office** (Urząd Gminy / Urząd Miasta) for your address.
  In Gdańsk this is the Urząd Miejski.
- You can register for a permanent (pobyt stały) or temporary (pobyt czasowy)
  stay at that address.
- It is **free**, and can often be handled together with a PESEL application.

## What to bring

- A valid passport or national ID card.
- A document proving your right to the dwelling — e.g. a tenancy agreement, or a
  confirmation from the property owner.
- The completed zameldowanie form.

## Note

Zameldowanie is about your **address**. It does not by itself grant or confirm
your right of residence as an EU citizen — that is the separate registration of
stay.
