# Driving licence for EU citizens in Poland

Driving licences issued by EU/EEA countries are valid in Poland, so most EU
citizens do not need to exchange their licence immediately.

## Using your existing EU licence

- An EU/EEA driving licence remains valid in Poland for as long as it is valid in
  the issuing country; you may drive on it without exchanging it.
- You may voluntarily exchange it for a Polish licence, and exchange becomes
  necessary when the document expires or if the authorities require an update of
  your registered data.

## Exchanging for a Polish licence

- The exchange is handled by the local district office (starostwo powiatowe) or
  city transport department (in cities, e.g. the Wydział Komunikacji).
- Commonly required: the current licence, a photo, an application form, and proof
  of residence in Poland. A medical certificate is generally not required just to
  exchange a valid EU licence.

## Non-EU licences

- Licences from outside the EU/EEA usually must be exchanged within a set period
  after establishing residence, sometimes with a theory exam, depending on the
  country and any bilateral agreement.

## Notes

- Penalty points and traffic fines are tracked against the driver in Poland once
  resident.
