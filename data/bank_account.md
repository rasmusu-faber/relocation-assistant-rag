# Opening a bank account in Poland

A Polish bank account (konto osobiste) makes salary payments, rent and bills much
easier and is effectively expected for everyday life.

## What you usually need

- A valid passport or national ID card.
- Proof of address or a PESEL number (requirements vary by bank; some open an
  account without a PESEL, others ask for one).
- Sometimes proof of the purpose of stay, such as an employment contract.

## Choosing a bank

- Large retail banks (e.g. PKO BP, Pekao, mBank, ING, Santander) offer accounts
  aimed at newcomers, often with an English-language app and online onboarding.
- Many personal accounts have no monthly fee if conditions are met (e.g. a minimum
  monthly card spend or incoming salary).

## Process

1. Apply online or in a branch; identity is verified in person or via a video/app
   check.
2. Receive the account number (IBAN starting with PL) and a debit card by post.
3. Activate online/mobile banking, which most Polish banks support in English.

## Notes

- BLIK is a widely used Polish mobile-payment standard tied to your bank app.
- Employers pay salaries by bank transfer, so having an account early is helpful.
