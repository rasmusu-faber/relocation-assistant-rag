# Social security (ZUS) in Poland

ZUS (Zakład Ubezpieczeń Społecznych) is Poland's Social Insurance Institution. It
collects social-security contributions that fund pensions, disability, sickness
and — together with NFZ — health insurance.

## As an employee

- On an employment contract (umowa o pracę), your employer registers you with ZUS
  within 7 days of your start date and pays contributions from your gross salary.
- Contributions cover retirement (emerytalne), disability (rentowe), sickness
  (chorobowe) and accident insurance, plus the health contribution routed to NFZ.
- Registration requires your PESEL number.

## As self-employed (B2B)

- If you run a sole proprietorship, you register yourself with ZUS and pay monthly
  contributions yourself.
- New entrepreneurs can use relief schemes such as the 6-month "start relief"
  (ulga na start) and later reduced "preferential ZUS" contributions.

## PUE ZUS online account

- The PUE/eZUS online platform lets you view contributions, sick-leave records
  (e-ZLA) and certificates, and handle most matters without visiting an office.

## Sickness and benefits

- After a qualifying period, ZUS sickness insurance provides paid sick leave and
  related benefits; an employer typically pays the first days, ZUS the remainder.
