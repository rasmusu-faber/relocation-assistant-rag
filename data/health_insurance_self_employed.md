# Health insurance when self-employed (JDG / B2B)

If you run a sole proprietorship (jednoosobowa działalność gospodarcza) or work
B2B, you are responsible for your own health insurance — no employer does it for
you.

## Registering and paying yourself

You register with ZUS as a contribution payer when you set up the business, and
you pay the health contribution (składka zdrowotna) to ZUS **every month**
yourself, together with your social contributions.

## How the amount is set

For the self-employed the health contribution is **income-dependent** and tied to
your chosen form of taxation (tax scale, flat tax, or lump-sum). It is
recalculated from your business income, so it varies rather than being a fixed
employer-deducted amount.

## Coverage

Once you are registered and paying, you and your registered family members have
access to NFZ care.

## Note

B2B contracts often show higher gross pay precisely because these contributions
are not employer-paid — you carry them yourself.
