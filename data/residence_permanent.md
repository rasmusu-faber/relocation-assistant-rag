# Right of permanent residence (after 5 years)

After living **legally and continuously in Poland for 5 years**, an EU citizen
acquires the **right of permanent residence** — a stronger status than the initial
registration of stay.

## Confirming it

You can apply at the **Voivodeship Office** (Urząd Wojewódzki) for a **document
confirming the right of permanent residence** (dokument potwierdzający prawo
stałego pobytu). It certifies a right you already hold by virtue of the 5 years —
the office confirms it rather than granting it.

## Continuity

The 5 years must be continuous. Short absences (holidays, or longer absences for
work or other important reasons within set limits) generally do not break
continuity.

## How it differs

- **Registration of stay** = the one-off step for stays over 3 months.
- **Permanent residence** = the status after 5 years, which no longer depends on
  being employed or having sufficient funds.
