# EU registration of stay (over 3 months)

As an EU citizen you may stay in Poland for up to 3 months freely. For a stay
**longer than 3 months** you should register your stay — this confirms your right
of residence, and is separate from address registration (zameldowanie).

## Where

At the regional **Voivodeship Office** (Urząd Wojewódzki). For the Pomeranian
region (including Gdańsk) this is the **Pomorski Urząd Wojewódzki**.

## What to bring

- A valid passport or national ID card.
- Proof of the **purpose of stay**, depending on your situation:
  - employment (a contract or employer statement), or
  - self-employment, or
  - study/enrolment, or
  - proof of sufficient funds **and** health insurance (if you are not working).
- The completed registration-of-stay application.

## What you get

A **registration certificate** confirming your right of residence as an EU
citizen. This is a one-off registration, not an annual renewal.
