# Health insurance (NFZ) in Poland

Public healthcare in Poland is run by the National Health Fund (Narodowy Fundusz
Zdrowia, NFZ). Access is normally tied to insurance contributions.

## Through employment

If you work in Poland on an employment contract (umowa o pracę), your employer
registers you with ZUS (Social Insurance Institution) and pays the health
contribution. This gives you and registered family members access to NFZ care.
A PESEL number is required for this registration.

## Self-employed or B2B

People working on a B2B basis (self-employed) pay their own ZUS and NFZ
contributions monthly. B2B contracts often pay more gross but do not include
employer-paid social benefits.

## Choosing a primary doctor

Once insured, register a declaration (deklaracja) with a primary-care physician
(lekarz POZ) at a chosen clinic to access routine care.

## EHIC in the transition period

Before your Polish insurance is active, your European Health Insurance Card
(EHIC) from your home country covers necessary medical care during the
transition.
