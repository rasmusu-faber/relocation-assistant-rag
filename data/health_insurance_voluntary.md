# Voluntary NFZ health insurance (dobrowolne ubezpieczenie)

If you are **not** covered through a job, through self-employment, or as someone's
family member — for example you are economically inactive or between jobs — you
can still get NFZ coverage by taking out **voluntary** health insurance.

## How it works

You sign a contract (umowa) directly with your regional NFZ branch (Wojewódzki
Oddział NFZ) and then pay a monthly contribution yourself, calculated on the
average national wage.

## Sign-up fee after a gap

If you have been without insurance for a longer period, the NFZ may charge a
one-off **entry fee** (a back-payment) proportional to how long you were
uninsured, before the contract starts. Short gaps usually carry no or only a small
fee.

## After signing

Once the contract is active and contributions are paid, you have NFZ access like
any insured person, and you can register your dependents.

## Note

This is the fallback route for people who have no other insurance title — not the
normal path for employees or entrepreneurs.
