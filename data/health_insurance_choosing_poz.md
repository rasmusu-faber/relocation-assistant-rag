# Choosing a primary-care doctor (lekarz POZ)

Being insured gives you the **right** to NFZ care, but to actually use routine care
you must choose a primary-care provider. This page assumes you are already insured.

## The declaration of choice

You pick a clinic (przychodnia) and submit a **declaration of choice** (deklaracja
wyboru) naming your primary-care doctor (lekarz POZ) and nurse. You can submit it
at the clinic or online through the Internet Patient Account (IKP) on
pacjent.gov.pl.

## What this gives you

Your chosen lekarz POZ is your first point of contact: check-ups, prescriptions,
referrals to specialists, and basic diagnostics.

## Changing your choice

You may change your POZ provider; the first two changes per calendar year are
free, after which a small fee normally applies.

## Note

Choosing a POZ doctor is separate from *being insured*: you need the insurance
first (through work, self-employment, voluntary insurance, or as a family member),
then you file the declaration.
