# PESEL number for EU citizens in Poland

The PESEL is an 11-digit national identification number used in Poland for
taxes, healthcare, banking and most administrative procedures.

## Who needs one

Anyone living in Poland who deals with public administration in practice needs a
PESEL. EU citizens settling in Poland (for work, study or family) should obtain
one soon after arrival.

## How to apply

1. Go to the municipal office (Urząd Gminy / Urząd Miasta) of the place where you
   live. In Gdańsk this is the Urząd Miejski w Gdańsku.
2. Submit the application form for assignment of a PESEL number, stating a legal
   basis (e.g. employment, registration of residence, tax obligations).
3. Bring a valid passport or national ID card, and supporting documents for the
   stated basis (e.g. an employment contract or a tenancy agreement).
4. The number is usually assigned on the spot or within a few days, free of charge.

## Notes

- A registered address (zameldowanie) often serves as a sufficient legal basis.
- The PESEL is required before you can register for public health insurance (NFZ)
  through an employer.
